lazy loading
   is a performance trick where a website or app waits to load pictures, 
   videos, or content until you actually look at them, rather than loading everything all at once
   How It Works (The Restaurant Analogy)Eager Loading (The Old Way): Imagine a waiter dumping 20 plates of food on your table 
   all at once, even though you can only eat one at a time. It clutters the table and takes forever to bring out.
   Lazy Loading (The Smart Way): The waiter brings you the first dish. When you finish it and look for more, they bring out 
   the next one.Real-World Example on a WebsiteWhen you open a long webpage with 50 pictures, your browser only loads the 
   pictures at the very top that you can see right away.The pictures further down stay blank or hidden.As you scroll down, 
   the website quickly loads each picture right before it appears on your screen.
   Why Is It Useful?Faster speeds: The page opens much quicker because your device has less work to do upfront.
   Saves data: If you leave the page before scrolling to the bottom, you don't waste internet data downloading pictures you never saw.
