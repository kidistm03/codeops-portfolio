About Habesha Market

Habesha Market is a fictional Ethiopian marketplace. We provide quality Ethiopian products like coffee, honey, spices (Berbere), injera, traditional clothes and also quality leather products. Our goal is to share Ethiopian culture with everyone and to pleasure our customers with quality products
